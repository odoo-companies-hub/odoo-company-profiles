About Odoo Company Profiles

Odoo Company Profiles is an independent directory that lists official Odoo implementation companies alongside their published contact details — company name, email, phone number, office address, and certification tier (Gold / Silver / Ready).

Where the data comes from

Every entry is pulled from that company's own public listing on Odoo's official partner directory (odoo.com/partners). We don't invent or guess contact details — if a company hasn't published a phone number or email on its own profile, this site shows "Not published" rather than filling in a placeholder. The email and phone shown are each company's own general published contact, not a specific department's direct line unless the company has listed it that way.

Why this exists

Businesses evaluating Odoo implementation partners often need a quick way to compare companies by location and tier, and to find a working contact without digging through multiple profile pages. This directory collects that information in one searchable place.

Corrections

If any listing is outdated, incorrect, or a company would like its information removed, please get in touch via the contact page.
